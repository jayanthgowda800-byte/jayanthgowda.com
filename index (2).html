About

A bit about me

I'm a Key Account Manager & Business Controller at Toyoda Gosei South India, managing key OEM accounts including Toyota, Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai, and VinFast. I specialize in driving strategic growth, product costing, pricing, and profitability, and enabling management decisions through data-driven insights.

I also lead long-term sales planning, budgeting, market analysis, and economic analysis to guide strategic account decisions, giving insights to management, and work closely with production to keep costing and delivery aligned with OEM build schedules.

Beyond work, I'm passionate about travelling & trekking, and maintaining an active lifestyle through regular workouts and gym training. I also enjoy cricket, music, and design — reflecting my belief in discipline, creativity, and balance.
